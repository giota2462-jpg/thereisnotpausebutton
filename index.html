There Is No Pause Button!

Created by Scott Cawthon • Released: May 16, 2013

About the Game

There Is No Pause Button! is an action-packed 2D auto-runner developed by Scott Cawthon for Android devices in May 2013[span_0](start_span)[span_0](end_span). Unlike traditional platformers, your character never stops moving. You must navigate through increasingly difficult obstacles, spikes, and traps with pixel-perfect precision[span_1](start_span)[span_1](end_span).

Controls & Mechanics

  • Tap Screen: Jump[span_2](start_span)[span_2](end_span)
  • Hold Screen: Higher Jump[span_3](start_span)[span_3](end_span)
  • Pause Menu: Non-existent! As the title suggests, there is no way to pause the game once a level starts[span_4](start_span)[span_4](end_span).
